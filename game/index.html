FIELD MANUAL / 01

HOLD THE LINE.

Your bunker covers the middle third of the ground. Asteroids that land to either side do no damage.

AIM + FIRE

Slide to rotate the gun. Tap for a shot. Hold for rapid fire while charging with ordinary weapons. Lightning, gravity, freeze, repulser, asteroid turrets and thruster missiles wait to fire until you release. Nine red lights on the bunker fill from the middle outward and pulse when ready. After 5 seconds, release a radial energy blast from the bunker that deals 4 damage to each target it reaches.

WEAPON CRATES

All weapons collect glowing diamond crates on contact, including lightning, chains and damaging shockwaves. Crates landing on your bunker are collected too. Each collected crate advances your weapon. Missed upgrades can drop again. Blue SHIELD crates add a circular transparent dome that absorbs three impacts, alongside your current weapon. Collecting another refreshes its three charges. AUTO TURRET crates deploy a weak gun on a ground flank. It fires three-shot bursts automatically. A second deploys on the opposite flank; only two can be placed per run. Any enemy impact destroys a turret, and UFOs target them too.

WEAPON PROGRESSION

Single → triple → bouncing shots → swarming missiles → chain lightning → gravity gun → freeze ray → acid rounds. Triple fires across a 15° spread; bouncing shots fire at half that rate, keep that spread and reflect off the walls and ceiling, stopping at the ground or bunker. Each swarm fires five tiny missiles. Aim within 3° of a target to lock on at launch: the missiles fan out, briefly slow, then accelerate hard toward it. Unlocked missiles wander and slow down. Faster missiles leave longer exhaust trails that fade over half a second. Chain lightning gives five shots, with three branching tiers dealing 8, 4, then 2 damage. Each shot produces one main lightning strike with fine cosmetic tendrils, increasing in number on smaller branches and tapering to nothing. It strikes immediately with long branches, a white flash, and a 0.4-second time freeze and recovery, then returns to missiles after its fifth use. Lightning fires once on release; holding only charges the bunker blast. The gravity gun gives five shots, firing once on release. Holding only charges the boost. A hit creates a black hole that pulls threats and loose debris within half the screen width into its center for about three seconds, then fades. Its white glow surrounds infalling debris that heats from black through red, orange, and yellow to white. Once spent, it returns to missiles. The freeze ray gives six shots on release in a cold blue 40° fan. Frozen threats stop moving and attacking; the next shot completely shatters them. Asteroid collisions shatter frozen rocks too. The acid shotgun fires seven green pellets in a roughly 36° fan every 0.75 seconds. Its pellets dissolve wireframe lines outward from each impact and deal damage over 0.75 seconds. After gravity is unlocked, crates cycle through freeze, acid, lightning, gravity, seekers, repulser, asteroid turrets, chain shot and thruster missiles. Upgrades preserve held input and your charge.

REINFORCEMENTS

FIGHTER DRONE bonuses send a tiny jet on a twenty-second patrol. It fires one-pixel rounds in bursts of six. MINELAYER bonuses launch an armored craft from the pickup location to place ten contact mines, then leave. Red spiked mines blink and drift in a slow, turbulent orbit around their drop point. Mines explode on contact with hostile threats and damage nearby threats. Neither bonus changes your current weapon. SHIELD II upgrades your dome to two layers and six impacts. TURRET II replaces your ground guns with triple-shot turrets that survive three hits each.

SPECIAL WEAPONS

SEEKER BALLS give twenty shots. Spiky rounds curve toward nearby threats and explode for 10 damage on contact. REPULSER gives one shot on release: its expanding energy front completely destroys every threat it crosses. ASTEROID TURRETS give three shots on release. Hit an asteroid to freeze it in place and install an autonomous gun. Your weapons pass through these friendly rocks. They stay until another asteroid, comet, UFO or torpedo collides with them. They do not hold up the next wave. CHAIN SHOT fires two weighted projectiles connected by a flexible chain. The weights pull it open and swing under tension and gravity; any hostile threat struck by a projectile or link explodes completely. THRUSTER MISSILES give ten shots on release. A thin, shimmering red laser sight shows your firing direction. Hit an asteroid to attach a visible engine that accelerates it off-screen, earning its normal score when it leaves. All upgrades keep your charge.

EXPANDED ARSENAL

RAILGUN fires on release: holding increases damage and penetration. FLAK fires on release: longer holds set a higher burst altitude, showering short-lived fragments. PRISM LASER sustains a beam while held and splits into three weaker rays on impact. SINGULARITY TETHER fires on release and pulls two threats near your aim together until they collide. RICOCHET DISC cuts through enemies, bounces off edges, then returns to the bunker. PLASMA BRAND marks its target; when that target dies, the charge jumps to a nearby threat with increased damage. These weapons join the late-game crate cycle.

BUNKER SYSTEMS

INTERCEPTOR BATTERY automatically launches rockets at the most urgent incoming torpedoes. SALVAGE MAGNET pulls crates within 220 units toward the bunker. REACTIVE ARMOR absorbs one unshielded bunker impact and retaliates with an upward shrapnel cone; another crate reloads it. OVERDRIVE CAPACITOR grants four seconds of faster fire and quicker recoil recovery whenever a full charge releases its radial blast. These four upgrades stack with your current weapon and last for the run, except spent armor.

DEBUG MODE

Open DEBUG SETUP before deploying. Choose all weapons and boosts, or select a test list. Your setup is remembered on this device. During a debug run, TEST lets you switch weapons with unlimited ammo and activate your selected boosts. Debug runs do not change your best score. Choose Normal game in setup to restore regular progression.

NEW THREATS

Comets streak toward the bunker once or twice per wave. They take 4 ordinary hits and deal 20 damage. A UFO enters lower as waves get harder and fires barrages of photon torpedoes. Each torpedo takes one hit to destroy and deals 2 damage to the bunker; the UFO takes 8 hits.
