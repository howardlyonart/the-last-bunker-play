How to play

Keep your bunker alive while the sky fills with asteroids, comets and UFOs. Shoot upgrade crates to unlock wild new weapons and support systems.

  • ← → or A D to aim
  • SPACE to fire
  • P to pause
  • Hold fire for five seconds, then release for a charged bunker blast.
